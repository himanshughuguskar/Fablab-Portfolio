Beginner

Sketching — 2D Basics

Master sketch tools: Line, Circle, Arc, Rectangle. Learn constraints — the backbone of parametric design: Coincident, Parallel, Perpendicular, and Dimensions.

Line, Circle, Arc Rectangle Tool Constraints (Coincident, Parallel) Dimensions
Practice:

3
Day 03
Intermediate

Advanced Sketch Practice

Level up with Offset, Trim, Extend tools. Master Mirror & Pattern for symmetrical designs. Tackle complex sketches that push your skills.

Offset & Trim Extend Tool Mirror & Pattern Complex Sketches
Practice:

Draw a gear outline (simple) and a bracket shape

4
Day 04
Core 🚀

3D Modeling — Core Day 🚀

Convert sketches to 3D! Master Extrude and Revolve operations. Apply Fillet and Chamfer modifications to create professional-looking parts.

Extrude Revolve Fillet Chamfer
Practice:

Make a Cylinder and a Bolt-like shape

5
Day 05
Intermediate

Assembly & Motion

Understand Components vs Bodies. Use the Joint tool to connect parts. Run Motion Studies to simulate real-world movement.

Components vs Bodies Joint Tool Motion Study Assembly Workflow

Create a simple hinge mechanism

Day 06
Advanced

Create real engineering design: Switch. Learn the Measure tool and Inspect features for precision work.
